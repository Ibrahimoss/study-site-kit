# Music for the shorts (optional)

You do not need anything here. Without music files, every short gets its own background music, made by the
renderer itself, in a style that matches its look.

If you prefer real tracks:

1. Put one or more music files you are allowed to reuse in this folder (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.ogg`,
   `.flac`). Free-to-use music libraries are a good source.
2. Render the shorts again. Each short picks one of the tracks and plays its own part of it, quietly under the
   voice.

On the site, viewers can mute the music and keep the voice. Music files here are never committed (see
`.gitignore` here), so they stay on your computer.
